# Webmaxxers Pricing Rules (DEMO)

Every amount and every page allowance comes from the price catalog, never from this text. This file explains how the numbers are used; it deliberately contains none, so updating prices never leaves it out of date.

## How a website price is built
Every project starts from a website type. The website type's base price covers a fixed number of pages (its included pages). Pages beyond the included count are priced individually by page type. Articles, the resource hub and add-ons are always priced separately and never count toward the included pages.

## Which pages fill the included allowance
Core pages fill the included allowance first, then recommended pages, in the order they appear in the sitemap. A page that falls outside the allowance is charged at its page type price (Standard, Service, Location, Advanced or Legal).

## Upgrading the website type
When a blueprint needs many pages, a larger website type can be cheaper than the smaller one plus many extra pages. Always recommend the cheaper option that covers the selected pages, and say so plainly.

## Content pricing
The Resource Hub Setup is required once before any articles are published, and each article is priced separately. Recommend articles in clusters that each support a specific service page, never as a generic "blog package".

## Add-on pricing
Add-ons have a one-time setup fee and, for some, a monthly fee. The monthly total is shown separately as ongoing services and is never folded into the initial investment.

## What the estimate always shows
Website base, extra pages (count and cost), content, add-on setup, total initial investment, and ongoing monthly cost. Future-opportunity items are listed but not included in the total unless the customer turns them on.
