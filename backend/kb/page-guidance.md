# Page & Feature Recommendation Guidance (DEMO)

## Recommendation principles
Never recommend a page or feature only because Webmaxxers sells it. Every recommendation must answer "why does this particular business need this?" and cite evidence from the intake or research. It is acceptable and expected to say "not recommended" with a reason.

## Choosing the five core pages
Pick the five pages that give the strongest launch foundation for this specific business. Contractor: Home, About, Services, Projects, Contact. Restaurant: Home, Menu, Catering, About, Contact. B2B tech: Home, Solution, Platform, Case Studies, Contact. Clinic: Home, Services, Providers, Patient Info, Contact.

## When a service deserves its own page
Give a service its own page when people search for it separately, it has its own buying decision, or it is a priority revenue line. Group minor services as sections on the Services overview instead of thin pages.

## When location pages are justified
Only recommend location pages when the business genuinely serves multiple distinct areas and can supply local proof (projects, reviews, local rules). Never create near-duplicate city pages.

## Proof and trust pages
High-ticket and trust-sensitive businesses (contractors, medical, legal, finance) need visible proof: licensing, insurance, certifications, warranties, reviews, before/after projects, case studies and team information.

## Separate FAQ page
Usually not recommended. Keep FAQs on the relevant service pages where they answer objections at the moment of decision.

## AI customer service chat
Recommend when the business receives many repetitive questions, gets after-hours inquiries, or has complex service routing. Not recommended when the conversion is a simple booking or a single call; a clear booking flow is cleaner.

## Managed forms
Recommend for any business whose primary conversion is a form submission (quote, estimate, consultation request), because lost leads directly cost revenue.

## Booking vs. quote system
Booking fits fixed-duration services (consultations, appointments, inspections). A quote system fits variable-scope work that needs details or photos before pricing.

## Content strategy
Plan article clusters around real customer questions: cost guides, problem/decision guides (repair or replace?), and regional/seasonal topics. Each article links to one related service page and its CTA.

## Content boundary
Plan what information, proof and conversion elements belong on each page. Do not prescribe visual styling.
