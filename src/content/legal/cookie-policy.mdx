## What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed by your browser when you visit a
website. They store data such as session identifiers, preferences, and
analytics.

## How We Use Cookies

- Ensure core site functionality (e.g., form submissions and security).
- Remember your preferences (language, theme, accessibility settings).
- Measure and analyze site traffic (only with your consent).

## Categories You Control

Our cookie banner uses the same categories as Google Consent Mode v2, so the
choice you make here is exactly the signal sent to Google:

- **Strictly Necessary** (`security_storage`) — Essential for the site to
  operate securely. Always on, and cannot be switched off.
- **Functionality** (`functionality_storage`) — Remembers choices such as your
  language, theme, and accessibility settings.
- **Analytics** (`analytics_storage`) — Lets us count visits and traffic
  sources. Until you allow this, Google Tag Manager is not loaded at all.
- **Advertising** (`ad_storage`, `ad_user_data`, `ad_personalization`) — Would
  allow advertising and measurement partners. We do not currently run
  advertising tags; this category keeps any future ones blocked unless you opt
  in.

Everything except Strictly Necessary is denied until you choose. You can change
or withdraw your choice at any time using the **Your Privacy Choices** link in
the footer, and withdrawing takes effect immediately.

## Cookies Set Regardless of Consent

We use **Vercel Analytics** and **Speed Insights** to measure page performance.
These are privacy-preserving and run on every page load: they set no cookies,
do not track you across sites, and do not collect your name, email, or IP
address. Because they do not store or read information on your device, they do
not require consent.

## Managing Cookies

Use the **Your Privacy Choices** link in the footer to change your categories.
You can also disable cookies via your browser settings, though some features
may not work correctly if you do.
